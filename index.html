Medit Link Express (Mac) is designed to bring structured and consistent scanning execution into a clinic's primary workstation environment.

It provides clinics with the flexibility to build and run scanning workflows in a way that best fits their practice, supported by stable and high-performance Mac hardware.

Key Benefits
  • Main Workstation Optimization
    Optimized for execution in the clinic's primary workstation
  • Reduced Training & Dependency
    Consistent execution regardless of the operator
  • Reliable and Standardized Daily Performance
    Repeatable and reliable scanning results
  • High-Volume Workflow Ready

Medit Link Express for Mac is offered as a paid software solution:

  • One-time Purchase License$2,000
